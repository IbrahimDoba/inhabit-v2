# Teen and grown turnaround prompts

Both use the complete style bible from ART-PLAN.md section 1. The approved
`assets/approved/dragon-young-sheet.png` is the exact identity/style reference.

Teen: tall lanky adolescent, head one fifth of total height, long neck, slightly
longer snout, bigger wings than the young reference, longer gently curved gold
horns, confident kind grin. Clearly a teenager, still cute.

Grown: mature adult, broad chest, strong long neck, large mint wings folded
loosely like a cloak, long gently twisted gold horns, calm wise friendly eyes,
a few tiny gold jaw spikes. Clearly larger and more mature than the teen,
never fierce.

Layout for each: exactly four separate full-body views in one horizontal row:
front, three-quarter, full side, back. Upright on two hind legs with two shorter
forearms, never quadrupedal. No accessories. Identical anatomy and scale across
views, whole horns/wings/tail inside each quarter. Pure flat white background,
no shadows, labels, text, borders or checkerboard. Wide landscape PNG, highest
available resolution. The saved model output's side views face left; runtime
walking and watering strips explicitly face right.
