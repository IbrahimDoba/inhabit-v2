# Codex shop art delivery

54 final PNGs: 20 plants, 12 decor, 10 house furniture, 12 badges. Built-in image generation with existing art references; native-alpha cleanup and trimming. No app or native project edits. No commits.

Skipped: `assets/images/decor/birdhouse-night.png`. Two generation attempts changed the roof perspective; both raw attempts are retained, but neither is shipped. No other known weak assets.

Accepted night variants use the day alpha stencil and identical canvas dimensions. All final PNGs are RGBA, have transparent padding, and were visually reviewed on mid-green contact sheets.
